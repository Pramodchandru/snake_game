# Pramod: Island Survival 🏝️

A top-down 2D island survival game built with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. Playable on both **Desktop** and **Mobile Phones / Tablets**!

## 🎮 Play Live
👉 **[Play Island Survival Live](https://pramodchandru.github.io/snake_game/)**

---

## 📱 Mobile Controls (Phone & Tablet)
The game includes a built-in virtual touch controller:
- **Virtual Joystick (Left):** Drag to move in 360 degrees. Pull further out to sprint!
- **🏃 Sprint:** Tap to toggle sprint mode.
- **⚔️ Attack:** Tap or hold to swing / hunt / fight zombies.
- **🖐️ Use:** Gather wood from trees, mine rocks, pick berries, drink from lakes, cook at fires.
- **🍖 Eat:** Consume berries or cooked meat to recover hunger and health.
- **🗡️ Spear:** Craft a spear (3 wood + 2 stone) for double attack power.
- **🪵 Fire:** Craft a campfire (5 wood) to keep zombies away and cook meat.
- **📱 Touch UI:** Toggle touch controls on/off anytime from the top bar.

## ⌨️ Desktop Controls (Keyboard & Mouse)

| Action | Controls |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Move** | `Arrow Keys` or `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` |
| **Sprint** | Hold `Shift` |
| **Attack / Swing** | `Spacebar` or `Left Click` |
| **Interact / Gather** | `E` (Chop trees, mine rocks, pick berries, drink from lakes) |
| **Eat Food** | `F` (Eat cooked meat or berries) |
| **Craft Spear** | `1` (3 wood, 2 stone - double damage & range) |
| **Craft Campfire** | `2` (5 wood - cooks meat & keeps zombies away) |
| **Pause / Resume** | `P` or `Esc` |
| **Start / Restart** | `Enter` or `Click / Tap` |

## 🌴 Survival Guide
- **Day & Night Cycle:** Daylight allows safe exploration. Night brings danger—craft campfires and watch out for zombies!
- **Vital Stats:** Manage Health, Hunger, Thirst, and Stamina.
- **Resource Gathering:** Chop trees for wood, mine boulders for stone, and pick bushes for berries.
- **Hunting & Cooking:** Hunt rabbits for raw meat and cook them at campfires for maximum nutrition.
- **Goal:** Survive for 5 days on the island!
